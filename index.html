About me

I'm passionate about games and interactions. Games let me explore new worlds, see through other people's eyes and step into stories beyond my own. As a designer, I want to make experiences that spark curiosity, stir emotions and stay with players long after they stop playing. I love turning an idea into something people can explore and connect with in their own way, and understanding what makes a game work so I can build my own.
