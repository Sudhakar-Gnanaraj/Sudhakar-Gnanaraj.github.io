Orbital Defense 2

Overview

Orbital Defense is a 2D top-down tower-defense game. You defend Earth by placing satellites on orbit rings and shooting down waves of incoming enemies. Each satellite can swing along its orbit. You spend currency on building and boosting them.

Core gameplay

Orbit building

Place satellites in fixed slots on orbit rings around Earth. Each satellite swings independently within its own arc.

Game economy

Earn parts from kills. Spend them on satellites and boosts, or sell towers for a refund.

Waves

A data-driven wave spawner with gaps between waves.

Progression

A level-select screen with unlockable levels (12 levels). Satellites and parts carry over between levels.

Game speed and pause

Speed control and a pause menu.
