Minefield Scanner

A playable pass at the “Minefield Scanner” mini-game event — built from the mechanics one-pager and UI screen spec, sections 1–13.

§4

Core loop

Every spin banks 1 Scanner Point. Spend points to tap tiles, read the clue color, and bank every cache before you find the mine.

§5

Entry

Cross 4,000 ★ to unlock the icon. First tap opens the briefing screen; every tap after that jumps straight to the sector map.

§7

Escalation

6 sectors, 3×3 → 6×6. Caches and mines both climb by 1 each sector, from a safe zero-mine opener up to a 6-cache, 5-mine finale.

§9

The pinch

No IAP inside the mini-game — Scanner Points only come from spinning. Run dry on Energy mid-event, and that's the upsell moment.

How to scan a sector

  1. Tap a tile — costs 1 Scanner Point.
  2. Read the color — green means no mine nearby, red means a mine is close, yellow means a mine AND a cache are both close.
  3. Deduce — a green tile means every tile next to it is safe to tap.
  4. Bank every cache before you find the mine.

Out of Energy

Spin again once Energy refills, or grab a pack now to keep scanning while the countdown runs.
