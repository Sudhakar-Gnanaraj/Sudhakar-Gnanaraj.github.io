Sections between checkpoints

Each row is the stretch a player replays after being caught. Select a row to jump the map there.

Geometry, guard settings, checkpoints and props are read directly from the Unity scenes (1 unit = 1 tile). Patrols use the game's own rules: 2 u/s walking speed, a pause at each end, sight range of 5 u at head and foot height, blocked by walls and barrels. Crouch passages are wherever the ceiling is one tile above the floor: the player stands 1.68 u tall and crouches to 0.76 u. Thrown-apple reactions are approximate.
