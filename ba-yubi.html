Yubi Bond App

Requirements and product planning for a new digital bond-selling application.

Overview Contribution Outcome

Overview

At Yubi, formerly CredAvenue, I worked on a new application for selling bonds. It was a product being defined from scratch, so my job was to work out what it needed to do before anything was built.

Details

Role
Business Analyst
Duration
November 2022 – January 2023
Product
Bond-selling application
Domain
FinTech, digital investment platforms, bonds

What I contributed

A foundation for a new product

I worked with stakeholders to understand the business problem and the target users, then structured the functionality they asked for into a clear product specification, including the business rules behind it.

Linking business goals and the user experience

I took the design team's proposed screens and flows and wrote the functional requirements behind them, connecting what the business wanted to achieve with what the product needed to do.

Work the developers could start on

I broke the product goals into prioritised epics and user stories, so scope, dependencies and delivery could be planned as the app moved into development.

Outcome

The project left a defined product for the bond-selling app: structured requirements, development-ready user stories and an agreed set of feature priorities, shared by business, design and engineering.
