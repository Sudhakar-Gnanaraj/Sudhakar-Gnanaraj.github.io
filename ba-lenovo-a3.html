Lenovo ThinkReality A3

Business analysis for an enterprise augmented reality ecosystem.

Overview Products How I worked Outcomes

Overview

ThinkReality A3 is Lenovo's enterprise augmented reality headset for productivity and immersive work. I was a Business Analyst across several apps in its ecosystem: A3 Home, ARCast and the A3 Companion App.

This was my longest and broadest product role. Beyond writing requirements, I helped plan releases, prioritise features across apps and keep teams in three countries working towards the same goals.

Details

Role
Business Analyst
Duration
March 2021 – August 2022
Product
Lenovo ThinkReality A3
Domain
Augmented reality, enterprise software, spatial computing

What I worked on

A3 Home

The app at the centre of the A3 experience. I supported the multi-window feature, which expanded A3 Home from a single window to up to three, and planned requirements for optimising the productivity apps people use most on the headset: Google Chrome, Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and Microsoft Office.

ARCast

An app that streams AR content to other devices. I gathered and refined its requirements and aligned scope and delivery expectations to take it to a formal release.

A3 Companion App

The app that supports the headset experience. I worked on its feature requirements, including raycast-based input, turning interaction concepts from designers and engineers into technical requirements.

How I worked

Across three countries

Stakeholders, designers and engineers were spread across India, China and the United States. I was the bridge between them, keeping a shared understanding of goals across time zones and negotiating priorities when their needs competed.

Across several apps

With more than one app in the ecosystem, I managed and prioritised epics and user stories for each, set release milestones and tracked the dependencies between them.

Outcomes

  • Multiple releases across the A3 app ecosystem
  • A3 Home: one window to three
  • ARCast formal release
  • Productivity apps optimised for the headset
