Requirements and feature planning for the Events section of a national health app.

Overview Events section Outcome

Overview

H365 is an app from Singapore's Health Promotion Board. I worked as a Business Analyst on one part of it: the Events section.

Details

Role
Business Analyst
Duration
March 2023 – September 2023
Product
H365 application
Domain
Digital health and wellness, mobile apps, events

The Events section

I owned the requirements for the Events section, taking each feature from what stakeholders expected to user stories the developers could build.

1. Stakeholder expectations

Gathered what the Health Promotion Board needed the Events section to deliver, and pinned down gaps and dependencies early.

2. Design discussions

Worked through the proposed user flows and screens for Events with the designers.

3. User stories for development

Turned the agreed designs into user stories and functional requirements, then answered developers' questions as they built the features, planning delivery around project milestones.

Outcome

Stakeholders, designers and developers worked from one shared definition of the Events section, with a prioritised backlog that fitted the project's release plan.
